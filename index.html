Documentation for Azure's internal monitoring softwareOutage detection · Incident response · Content strategy

Challenge
The internal monitoring software had frequent outages, a detection system that produced false positives and unnecessary escalations, and slow incident response that increased downtime and hurt reliability.

Approach

  • Ideation and strategy: ran brainstorming sessions with software engineering, operations, and customer support to shape the content strategy and information architecture.
  • SWOT analysis: assessed strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in outage detection and the content process, and reflected the findings in the documentation.
  • User feedback: gathered input through meetings with front-end teams and surveys to build a content improvement roadmap.
  • Prioritization: used MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won't) to rank planned features, including improved alert thresholds, refined anomaly detection, and enhanced dashboard functionality.
  • Data-driven incident response: analyzed incident data and response trends to refine response protocols and reduce response times.
  • Workshops: organized collaborative workshops with engineering, operations, and support teams.

Result
Clearer documentation and a prioritized roadmap for faster, more reliable incident response.
